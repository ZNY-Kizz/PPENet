# Provisional data-use notice

This subset is prepared for non-commercial academic verification of PPENet.
Before public upload, the authors must obtain written disclosure approval from
the data provider and replace this provisional notice with the provider-approved
dataset license.

Permitted use is limited to research on knowledge graph representation,
completion, retrieval, and reproducibility. Users must not attempt to identify
or link released event nodes to real incidents, persons, organizations, or
locations. Redistribution, commercial use, operational decision-making, and
use for evaluating identifiable individuals are not authorized by this notice.

The raw source data and the complete EFKG-v1.0 graph remain restricted.
